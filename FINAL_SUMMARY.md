# 🎉 Cash Cow Extension - Final Implementation

## ✅ How It Works

### 1. **Checkout Detection**
- Automatically detects when you're on checkout pages
- Extracts purchase amount and merchant name
- Works on: Amazon, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Costco, Walmart, Shell, Chevron, Exxon

### 2. **Badge Notification** (Honey-style!)
- Shows orange "!" badge on extension icon
- Indicates a recommendation is ready
- No intrusive popups on the page

### 3. **Open Popup**
- **Click** extension icon in toolbar
- **OR** press **Ctrl+Shift+H** (Cmd+Shift+H on Mac)
- See your card recommendations!

### 4. **Smart Recommendations**
- Shows all 3 of your cards (Amex Gold, Chase Sapphire, Discover It)
- Highlights the BEST card for that purchase
- Explains why it's best with reward rates
- Calculates points you'll earn

### 5. **Features**
- Card carousel with swipeable navigation
- Points calculation
- Theme support (light/dark)
- Keyboard shortcut

## 📦 To Use

1. **Load extension** in `chrome://extensions/`
2. **Visit** any merchant site
3. **Add items** to cart
4. **See** orange "!" badge appear
5. **Click** extension icon or press Ctrl+Shift+H
6. **See** recommendation in popup!
